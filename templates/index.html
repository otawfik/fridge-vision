🪴 Fridge Vision

Snap a photo of your fridge. Fridge Vision detects what is inside with YOLOv8, scores freshness with a trained classifier, estimates days left, and suggests recipes for what you have.

How it works

  • Detection: YOLOv8n finds fridge items (fruit, drinks, leftovers).
  • Freshness: a logistic regression on color and texture features scores each item fresh vs spoiled.
  • Inventory: shelf-life table turns freshness into estimated days left, use-soon-first.
  • Recipes: TF-IDF retrieval matches your items against 39 curated recipes.

Run locally with python app.py, then open http://localhost:5000.
